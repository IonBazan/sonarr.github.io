Sonarr - Smart PVR for Usenet and Torrents

Sonarr is an internet PVR for Usenet and Torrents.

It can monitor multiple RSS feeds for new episodes of your favorite shows and will grab, sort and rename them. It can also be configured to automatically upgrade the quality of files already downloaded when a better quality format becomes available.

Download GitHub

Features

Calendar

See all your upcoming episodes in one convenient location.

Manual Search

Find all the releases, choose the one you want and send it right to your download client.

Automatic Failed Download Handling

Password protected releases, missing repair blocks or virtually any other reason? Sonarr will automatically block the release and try another one until it finds one that works.

Quality Profiles

Fully customizable quality profiles, and notifications for the services you already use.

Multiple Series Views

Browse your series as posters, an overview or a table.

Built-in Updater

See what's new without leaving the comfort of the app.
